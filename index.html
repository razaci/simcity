City life

Welcome to City Gods

Pick a tool and tap the map. Homes and businesses need a road beside them. All roads must connect to the starting streets.

Inspect a building to upgrade it. Homes attract residents; shops and factories provide jobs and income. Parks, clinics, power and water keep your city happy.

Each city day lasts 10 seconds. Drag to move the view; use +/− to zoom. Your city saves automatically when storage is available.
